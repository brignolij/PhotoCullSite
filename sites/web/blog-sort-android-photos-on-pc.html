How to sort your Android photos on a Windows PC

Jeffrey Brignoli · November 16, 2026

Using an iPhone? Read the iPhone guide: How to sort your iPhone photos on a Windows PC

Windows reads Android phones well, but reading is not sorting. With thousands of photos, the built-in tools leave you scrolling one long list of file names. Here is what they do, and a faster way to clear the phone from your PC.

With the tools built into Windows

  • File Explorer: after you choose “File transfer” on the phone, its DCIM folder shows up as files. You can delete from there, but with no grouping and no way to compare similar shots.
  • The Photos app: imports a copy to the PC. A good backup, but it doesn't tell you what to remove from the phone.
  • Phone Link: shows recent photos only, one at a time.

With PixCuller, step by step

1. Install PixCuller. Get it for free for Windows 10 or 11, or from the Microsoft Store. Nothing needs to be installed on the phone.

2. Connect by USB. Plug in the phone, unlock it and choose “File transfer” in its USB notification. Then click Android phone in PixCuller, and Connect over USB.

3. Sort trip by trip. The Trips view groups the camera roll by date and place and shows the space each trip takes. Start with the heaviest.

4. Compare and mark. Similar photos carry a badge: open up to six side by side, keep the best and mark the rest, along with screenshots and big videos.

5. Delete safely. Confirm, and PixCuller deletes the photos directly over the cable. The deletion is immediate and permanent on the phone (no trash), but by default PixCuller keeps a copy of each deleted item on your PC for 30 days, in a “PixCuller Trash” folder in Pictures.

Good to know

  • Everything stays on your PC and your phone: no account, no cloud, no upload.
  • Deleting from File Explorer is permanent with no copy kept; PixCuller keeps the 30-day copy on the PC by default.
  • PixCuller is free, with no daily limit, and sorting a folder on the PC works the same way.
